# Revenue Leakage & Pricing Intelligence System

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on identifying revenue leakage, pricing issues, excessive discounts, and loss-making products in an e-commerce business.

The project analyzes sales, profit, discounts, products, categories, and geographical performance to understand where revenue is being generated and where profitability is being lost.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business is generating strong sales, but management suspects that excessive discounts, low-margin products, and poor pricing decisions are reducing profitability.

The goal of this project is to identify the major sources of profit leakage and provide data-driven recommendations to improve profitability.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Excel** – Data cleaning and initial analysis
- **SQL (PostgreSQL)** – Data analysis and business queries
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization

## 📊 Key KPIs

- Total Sales: **$2.30M**
- Total Profit: **$286.40K**
- Profit Margin: **12.47%**
- Average Discount: **15.62%**
- Profit Leakage: **$77.07K**
- Loss-Making Products: **299**

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Technology is the best-performing category by sales and profit.
- Furniture has the lowest profit margin at **3.80%**.
- Tables and Bookcases are major loss-making sub-categories.
- Discounts above **30%** resulted in approximately **$125K in losses**.
- The analysis identified **299 loss-making products**.
- Texas has the highest loss among the analyzed states.
- Profit margin increased from **10.23% in 2014 to 13.43% in 2016**, before declining to **12.74% in 2017**.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Control discounts above 30% and use them only when justified.
2. Review pricing and discount strategies for Furniture, especially Tables and Bookcases.
3. Review loss-making products and consider repricing, reducing discounts, or discontinuing consistently unprofitable products.
4. Investigate loss-making states and cities to identify the causes of negative profit.
5. Evaluate promotions based on profit and margin rather than sales alone.
6. Monitor profit, margin, discounts, and profit leakage regularly.
